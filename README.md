### ⚔️ PATH TWO — See What He Has Built

*"Show me your work,"* you say. The torchlight brightens.

He gestures to three doors behind him.

---

**🗡️ Door One: [sql-of-the-ancients](https://github.com/Athility/sql-of-the-ancients)**

> You open the door. Inside is a dungeon — but the dungeon *is* a database. Every monster is a row. Every battle is a stored procedure. A full RPG engine, written entirely in MySQL 8.0+, with no application layer between you and the query.
>
> *You have never been more confused. You have never been more impressed.*

---

**☀️ Door Two: [dual-axis-solar-tracker](https://github.com/Athility/dual-axis-solar-tracker)**

> You open the door. A device sits on a pedestal, slowly rotating toward a light source above. An ATmega328P microcontroller pulses. Two servo arms adjust with quiet precision.
>
> *"It follows the sun,"* Atharva says. *"Because I told it to."*

---

**🪷 Door Three: [anantah](https://github.com/Athility/anantah)**

> You open the door. An artisan in rural India holds up a terracotta vase, photographed against a cluttered wall. A moment later: the same vase, studio-lit, background removed, listed in Hindi on a marketplace — AI-processed in real time, on a budget laptop, in a village with weak internet.
>
> *"Beauty of the Art Stays Eternal,"* reads the sign above the door.
